For developers

One API call to start a payment

Create an order, send the customer to the checkout URL we return, and wait for the webhook.

  • Simple REST API with an x-api-key header
  • Signed webhooks with timestamp and HMAC signature
  • Return-to-site redirect locked to your domain
Read the API docs 
curl -X POST https://taknapay.in/api/create-payment \
  -H "x-api-key: YOUR_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"amount": 99, "orderId": "ORDER_1001"}'

# → { "success": true,
#     "checkoutUrl": "https://taknapay.in/?orderId=ORDER_1001" }
FAQ

Questions? Answered.

How fast is verification?

Your request is verified instantly once you submit your PAN details, so you can start integrating right away. You can see the status in your dashboard.

What do I need to register?

Your name as on PAN, PAN number and a clear photo of the card, your business name, email and website address.

When do I get my money?

Each payment is added to your balance after the settlement period. Then request a payout by NEFT, IMPS or UPI from your dashboard.

I lost my login token. What now?

Contact the admin. They can issue a new login token for your account.

Do you also offer simple payment links?

Yes. If you do not have a website, use Payment Links instead.
