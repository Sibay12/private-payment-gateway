FAQ

Questions? Answered.

How fast is verification?

Send your request and it is verified instantly, so you can start creating links or integrating the API right away.

What do I need to register for Payment Links?

Your name, mobile number, email ID, a password and your PAN card details. That is all.

What does it cost?

Creating payment links is free. When a payment is received, 2% is charged, and withdrawing your money costs 1%.

How do I get my money?

Open your dashboard, request a payout by NEFT, IMPS or UPI, and we process it as fast as possible. The money you can withdraw settles 3 working days after payment.

Can I use TaknaPay.in on my own website?

Yes. Use Merchant onboarding, get your API key and follow the API docs.
