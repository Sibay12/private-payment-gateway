FAQ

Questions

How do payers pay?

They open your link, enter their name (and amount if it is not fixed) and pay with any UPI app by scanning the QR or tapping pay.

When can I withdraw?

After the settlement period shown above. Then request a payout to your bank account or UPI ID from the Withdraw page.

Why do you need my PAN?

Your PAN is used once to verify your identity before the account is approved. Only the admin can see the photo.
